intern-pipeline

intern-pipeline is a personal tool written and run by Hargen Zheng for his own job search. It is not a product or a service offered to anyone else; the only user is its author.

What it does

  • Watches public job boards and careers pages for research internship openings and sends the author a summary.
  • Reads the author's own Gmail mailboxes, read-only, to notice job-application emails such as confirmations, interview requests and rejections.
  • Keeps a ledger of the author's applications in a Google Sheet owned by the author.

The Google account access this tool requests is used only on the author's own accounts and only for the purposes above.
